Behind LeLuxe

Beauty that begins
with healthy skin.

LeLuxe Dermsolutions was built on a simple belief: real beauty should look like you, only more confident and radiant. Every plan begins with a doctor's consultation and a genuine assessment of your skin — never a template.

Led by Doc Genie, our clinic pairs advanced dermatological treatments with a warm, unhurried experience. From facials to our signature Juvelook skin booster, every service is chosen to enhance your skin's own quality and glow.
